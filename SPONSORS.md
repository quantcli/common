# Sponsors

Thank you to everyone who supports QuantCLI, a small open-source project for
exporting your own personal data.

You can support the project through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/quantcli).

Public acknowledgements use one alphabetical list, with each sponsor's chosen
name and tier and the same presentation for everyone. We publish names only
with permission and keep private sponsorships private. No roster is listed here yet.

Donations do not buy special influence over governance, the roadmap, or technical
decisions, or any promise of private support, priority service, or endorsement.
Sponsors are welcome to offer feedback on the same terms as anyone else.

See [BUDGET.md](BUDGET.md) for the project's funding and operating-cost policy.
