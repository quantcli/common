# Sponsors

QuantCLI is a small open-source project that maintains three CLIs for exporting
your own quantified-self data. Thank you to everyone who helps keep it running.

Our funding platform is [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/quantcli).

## What sponsorship means

Sponsorship supports continued operation and provides an optional public
acknowledgement on this page. All tiers receive the same acknowledgement format.

Sponsorship does not provide governance influence, roadmap input or priority,
contract or schema concessions, exclusive access, a support SLA, private support,
or product endorsement. This applies equally to individuals, companies, and
competing tool vendors. There are no private sponsorship terms or side agreements.
Funding does not determine technical decisions.

## GitHub Sponsors

The first roster reconciliation is pending. This page does not yet report sponsor
names, tier assignments, or an anonymous sponsor count. This is not a statement
that there are no sponsors.

After verification against GitHub Sponsors records and Executive Director review,
public acknowledgements will use one alphabetical list across all tiers, with
each sponsor's chosen public name and tier name only. There are no logos or
differences in placement, text size, or emphasis by tier.

Sponsors who choose not to be named will be counted together as `Anonymous (N)`.
Their identities and individual tier assignments will not be published. We will
not infer permission to name a sponsor from a payment record alone.

## Updates and corrections

This is the canonical sponsor acknowledgement page. Roster updates are checked
against GitHub Sponsors records and acknowledgement preferences, then reviewed
by the Executive Director before publication through a pull request.

For a correction, open an issue in
[quantcli/common](https://github.com/quantcli/common/issues). Do not put private
sponsor identities or payment records in a public issue. A sponsor can change
their public or private sponsorship setting through GitHub Sponsors.
