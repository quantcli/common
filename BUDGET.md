# QuantCLI budget

QuantCLI is a small open-source project for exporting your own personal data.
Funding comes through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/quantcli).

The operating spend ceiling is **$300 per month**. This is a limit, not actual
spending, a fundraising target, or a commitment to spend. Donations do not
automatically authorize new spending. Increasing the ceiling or taking on new
recurring costs requires maintainer approval.

Funding helps cover project hosting, agent runtime, domains, CI, and any platform
or payment fees. Sponsors receive no special influence or support rights; see
[SPONSORS.md](SPONSORS.md).

We keep supporting financial records privately and publish short summaries when
useful, using verified figures and redacted supporting evidence where appropriate.
Summaries distinguish project costs, founder-paid costs, and reimbursements.
Private sponsor identities and payment details stay private.

Corrections are welcome through an issue in
[quantcli/common](https://github.com/quantcli/common/issues). Please do not put
private financial records or sponsor information in public issues.
