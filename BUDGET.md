# QuantCLI budget

QuantCLI is a small open-source project that maintains three CLIs for exporting
your own quantified-self data. This page is the canonical record of project
funding and operating costs.

## Operating envelope

The operating spend ceiling is **$300 per month**. This is a policy limit, not
actual expenditure, a fundraising target, or a promise to spend.

Public approval record: [board review and publication PR](https://github.com/quantcli/common/pull/39). Increasing the
ceiling or taking on a recurring cost requires prior Executive Director and
board approval. Contributions do not automatically authorize new spending.

## Funding and independence

Our funding platform is [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/quantcli).
Public acknowledgements belong in [SPONSORS.md](SPONSORS.md).

Sponsors support continued operation. They do not buy governance influence,
roadmap input or priority, contract concessions, exclusive access, private
support, a support SLA, or product endorsement. These terms apply equally to
individuals, companies, and competing tool vendors. There are no private terms
or side agreements. Technical decisions do not depend on sponsorship.

## What operating costs cover

| Category | Evidence required for actual spending |
| --- | --- |
| Infrastructure and hosting | Provider invoice or receipt for project services |
| Agent runtime | Project-attributable usage and billing records |
| Domains and DNS | Registration or renewal receipt |
| CI and build services | Invoice for project charges |
| Platform and payment fees | Actual fee entries in primary platform records |

These categories describe costs that may be reported, not spending allocations
or new commitments. Founder-paid costs, reimbursements, and in-kind credits are
identified separately when evidenced. No fee percentage or reserve balance is
assumed.

## Monthly snapshots

The first report will be prepared in December 2026 following the November 9
GitHub Sponsors launch. Its reporting period and source cut-off will be stated
explicitly. The first reporting period is November 9–30, 2026, in UTC.

No verified snapshot has been published yet. Missing records do not establish
zero receipts, zero expenses, or a zero balance.

Each report will link public primary records for opening and closing balances,
GitHub Sponsors receipts, refunds, actual fees, other project payments, and the
reconciliation between them. Platform payouts will be identified as transfers
when appropriate, without counting them twice as income. Original currencies
and any evidenced conversions will be stated.

Reports will compare actual spend with the approved envelope, explain anomalies,
and link releases or other shipped artifacts for the period. Cost per shipped
artifact will use a stated counting rule and sourced totals. The first report
will be labelled as the first reporting period, without an invented prior-month
comparison.

Private sponsor identities and payment details will not be published. Public
source records must retain sufficient evidence to reproduce the reported totals.
Figures without an accessible primary source will be held until evidence is
available.

## Review and corrections

Sustainability prepares updates through pull requests. The Executive Director
reviews the text, source records, and figures before the board approval and merge
step. A monthly update does not authorize a change to sponsor rights or future
spending.

Report an error through an issue in
[quantcli/common](https://github.com/quantcli/common/issues), with `budget` in the
title and a link to the affected public source. Do not put private payment
records or sponsor identities in a public issue. File history is the audit trail.
